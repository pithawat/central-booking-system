Text (verbatim): "BAM". Exactly these three uppercase letters. No full department name, no BSSD, no One, no Booking, no Thai, no slogan, no captions, no concept number.
Design direction: a compact architectural emblem beside a bespoke BAM wordmark, sophisticated futuristic enterprise software branding. Create a strong distinctive capital B monogram built from two rounded reservation-slot shapes joined by a single shared vertical spine. The two open white/transparent interior counters should be clean and generous, and the lower counter contains a subtle integrated upward check-shaped negative-space cut, signifying a confirmed booking. It should suggest unified reservations through modular construction rather than depicting a literal detailed calendar. No surrounding calendar frame, no binding tabs, no grid of mini squares. Typography: BAM in a bespoke wide geometric sans-serif with slightly rounded corners and selective clean angular cuts that echo the emblem; bold confident proportional letters, precisely kerned, highly readable, no excessive stencil breaks. The A may have a subtle angular cut echoed from the confirmation shape. B A M must remain unmistakable.
Composition: one perfectly balanced horizontal logo lockup centered on a transparent canvas, emblem left, BAM right. Large enough to occupy roughly 80 percent of width. Emblem a little taller than wordmark. Very clean margins. No alternative examples, no brand board, no enclosing plaque or app tile.
Style: ultra-modern premium digital product identity, Swiss optical precision, strong bold silhouette, masterful negative space, flat vector aesthetic, razor-sharp edges, suitable for later vector reconstruction and mobile icon use.
Color palette: main emblem vivid cobalt #1D4ED8, a carefully controlled cyan #22D3EE accent only on a small part of the emblem, wordmark deep ink navy #0F172A. Solid flat colors only. Actual transparent background everywhere outside the graphic, including letter counters.
Avoid: red, pink, coral, orange, maroon, rainbow colors; gradients, 3D extrusion, metallic/chrome, glossy bevels, glass, glow, drop shadows, background shapes, mockups, watermarks, tiny flourishes, circuits, swooshes, leaves, globes, literal cars or meeting rooms, generic stock calendar/check icons, extra text. Beautiful logo, not an illustration.
